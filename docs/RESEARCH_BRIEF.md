# Muse Research Brief
## Mission and authorization
Research one property in Rosedale, Queens, New York, identified privately by the user. Do not guess its address. Begin with broad readily accessible public records and imagery, then targeted gap research. No purchases, account changes, outreach or publication without approval. Ask owner only when reasonable sources are exhausted. Keep private records and imagery secure; avoid unnecessary personal information about prior owners.

## Inventory
- Property: construction year, style, building area, lot dimensions/area, parcel boundaries, footprint, floors, exterior structures, terrain/elevation, driveway, fences, trees and landscaping.
- Financial: recorded sales, market estimates over time, annual taxes, assessments; optional inflation comparison. Do not collect private mortgage or lien details unless separately justified and approved.
- History: transfers, permits, additions, historical maps, aerial and street-level imagery, noteworthy property-related news, documented damage or accidents. Local storms/events must not be asserted to have affected property without evidence.
- Environment: flood zones, significant weather, water bodies, terrain/drainage, soil, known utility infrastructure, vegetation, sunlight and seasons.
- Owner information when needed: purchase details, renovations, improvement costs, issues, photos, roof/exterior maintenance and major repairs. Personal memories later and optional.
- Context: comparable sales, local values/tax trends, development and environmental risks, significant local history; no neighboring building modeling or unnecessary demographics.

## Sources and imagery
Identify municipality and official assessor/GIS, tax, land records and permit portals first; investigate state GIS, historical maps, aerial archives, reputable real estate sources and news archives. Google Maps/Street View may help identify visible features, but verify terms for storage, screenshots, API use, derivative modeling and display before collecting or incorporating restricted imagery. Prefer permitted sources and owner photos; use links where copying is not permitted. No paid APIs or records without approval. Approximate missing geometry and record uncertainty.

## Deliverables
Provide concise research summary, machine-readable structured records (JSON or CSV proposed), source index, permitted visual reference manifest, dated event list, geometry observations and prioritized gap backlog. Each record should include stable ID, category, value/unit, effective date or range, source URL/reference, retrieval date, evidence type, confidence, notes and rights restrictions where relevant. Separate estimates from actual sales and assessments; explain estimation methods and avoid unsupported precision. Preserve conflicting evidence instead of silently resolving it. Keep unknowns explicit. Store private data outside public assets/repositories.

## Stages and completion
1. Confirm property identity and discover sources.
2. Collect readily available geometry, imagery, essential records and broader inventory.
3. Reconcile records, identify changes visible between dated images and infer labeled date ranges.
4. Deliver initial research package, limitations, permissions and questions only owner can answer. Backlog obscure/offline records. No coding or ongoing monitoring yet.

Future monitoring: propose sources and suitable cadence for values, comparable sales, taxes, permits, imagery, relevant events and gaps after source discovery. No active schedules or automatic updates until separately approved.
