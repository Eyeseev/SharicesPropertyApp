## How to use
1. Paste the MASTER PROPERTY BLOCK + STYLE BLOCK together as the base of every prompt.
2. Add ONE concept block per generation. Keep the property block word-for-word identical across concepts so only viewpoint/composition varies.
3. Score each result with the scorecard at the end (per CREATIVE_DIRECTION: silhouette, placement, visual coherence, lighting, vegetation, UI legibility, rendering feasibility).

## MASTER PROPERTY BLOCK (use verbatim in every prompt)
A compact 1945 Cape Cod house, one-and-a-half storeys, long and narrow, on a flat rectangular corner lot. Ground floor walls are warm red-brown brick; the upper gables are pale warm-cream horizontal siding with white trim. The front of the house is a steep dark grey-brown shingled gable end facing a street corner, with a white arched front door with an oval glass insert and a stone surround under a small gable porch, reached by red-brown concrete steps with black metal railings. Behind the front gable, the main roof is lower-pitched and light-medium grey — a distinctly two-tone roof. One single red-brick chimney toward the rear of the house. White-framed double-hung windows; two ground-floor windows and two tiny barred basement windows on the long side elevation. At the rear, a small single-storey white extension steps the house down, then a concrete driveway leads to a small detached white single-car garage with a dark grey pitched roof at the far rear corner of the lot. A dark-red Japanese maple stands in the small front lawn. A black chain-link fence about four feet high encloses the two street frontages, with a gate at the corner aligned to the front walkway. Mature green trees canopy the corner and one side of the lot. No pool, no deck, no shed, no dormers, no second chimney, no attached garage.
